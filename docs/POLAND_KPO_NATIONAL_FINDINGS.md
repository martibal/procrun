# Poland KPO national-source findings

## Decision

Production eligibility as a Poland-wide project-discovery source: **REJECTED**.

The Krajowy Plan Odbudowy i Zwiększania Odporności (KPO) is current, nationally important and highly relevant to ProcRun's infrastructure scope. It is not, however, documented as one current Poland-wide project-level discovery surface that can replace the blocked SL2021 route.

## Official evidence reviewed

Current Ministry of Funds and Regional Policy material describes KPO as a national programme of reforms and investments with more than one million support agreements and investments spanning local infrastructure, energy, transport, social infrastructure and other sectors. Current official material also states that implementation responsibility is distributed across individual ministries and implementing bodies.

Official project/result publications found during this review are correspondingly fragmented by responsible institution and investment/action. Examples include ministry-specific project lists, ranking lists and individual implementing-body project pages rather than one authoritative national project feed.

This fragmentation matters before privacy or transport details are even considered: ProcRun needs a discovery source whose coverage can be bounded and tested nationally. A collection of institution-specific pages cannot be treated as complete Poland coverage without an authoritative national index or manifest proving what must be enumerated.

## Gates

- current KPO relevance: **PASS**
- infrastructure/commercial relevance: **PASS**
- authoritative Poland-wide project-level coverage surface: **FAIL / NOT ESTABLISHED**
- completeness boundary that can be tested automatically: **FAIL / NOT ESTABLISHED**
- field-bounded pre-receipt transport: **NOT INVESTIGATED** because coverage fails first
- rich-scope pre-publication zero-PII guarantee: **NOT INVESTIGATED** because coverage fails first
- project/list row smoke test: **PROHIBITED**
- production eligibility: **REJECTED**

## Safety consequence

No KPO project-list attachment, ranking-list body, project row or beneficiary record was fetched for this candidate. Search-result and official-page metadata were sufficient to establish the coverage architecture failure. ProcRun must not enumerate ministry or implementing-body project lists as though that created national completeness.

A future KPO route may be reopened only if an official source publishes a machine-enumerable national project index or equivalent authoritative manifest with a defined completeness boundary. Only then should RIGHTS, ACCESS, field-bounded transport and rich-scope zero-PII guarantees be evaluated.
