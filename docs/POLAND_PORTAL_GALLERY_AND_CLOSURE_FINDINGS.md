# Poland source research: public project gallery and close-by-default decision

## Candidate 7: Portal Funduszy Europejskich public project gallery

Official source reviewed:
- https://funduszeeuropejskie.gov.pl/

The official Portal Funduszy Europejskich homepage presents a public section labelled `Zobacz zrealizowane projekty` (see completed/realised projects) and surfaces selected project stories/examples.

### Gate result

- official government source: **PASS**
- project-level public web surface: **PASS in principle**
- temporal suitability for ProcRun discovery: **FAIL**
- Poland-wide completeness boundary: **FAIL / NOT ESTABLISHED**
- machine-testable current 2021-2027 discovery coverage: **FAIL / NOT ESTABLISHED**
- field-bounded pre-receipt transport: **NOT INVESTIGATED**
- rich-scope pre-publication zero-PII guarantee: **NOT INVESTIGATED**
- project/list-row smoke test: **PROHIBITED**
- production eligibility: **REJECTED**

The failure is deliberately taken at the cheapest gate. The portal itself frames this surface as completed/realised projects and presents project stories/examples. ProcRun needs current funded projects early enough to determine which purchasable components remain commercially open. A completed-project gallery is therefore structurally too late even before privacy or transport questions are reached.

The official national 2021-2027 project-list publication remains the authoritative broad-coverage route identified during this research. That route was already rejected because its broad downloadable distribution contains beneficiary/contractor identity fields and no authoritative pre-receipt column projection was established.

No project detail page, gallery listing body beyond already-public homepage material, API response, attachment, beneficiary record or project row was fetched for this candidate.

## Poland close-by-default decision

After seven materially distinct official-source architectures, Poland source discovery is **CLOSED BY DEFAULT** for the current product contract. This is not a claim that no useful Polish public project data exists. It means no reviewed official route currently satisfies the combined ProcRun requirements of current funded-project coverage, sufficiently rich project-specific scope, lawful/reusable access, and zero-PII safety before receipt.

Reviewed architectures now include:
1. national SL2021 2021-2027 project-list bulk files;
2. dane.gov.pl row API;
3. Mapa Dotacji UE;
4. CST 2021 implementation-contract/location report;
5. CUPT public project-list pages;
6. national KPO publication architecture;
7. Portal Funduszy Europejskich public completed-project gallery.

The recurring blockers are structural rather than incidental: broad national files include identity-bearing fields without pre-receipt projection; public web/list routes either expose identity-bearing material, lack a provable completeness boundary, or are editorial/completed-project surfaces rather than current discovery feeds; and the KPO architecture is institutionally distributed rather than an authoritative Poland-wide project index.

### Reopen conditions

Poland may be reopened only when genuinely new official evidence establishes a source or contract that simultaneously provides:
- a current and explicit coverage boundary suitable for ProcRun;
- machine-usable access with acceptable reuse rights;
- authoritative field-bounded output before receipt, excluding all natural-person identity fields;
- sufficiently rich project-specific scope for exact component evidence spans;
- an explicit pre-publication zero-PII guarantee for any retained rich free text.

Until those conditions are met, no Poland project-row or broad-file probing is permitted and no Poland project source may be registered as live.
