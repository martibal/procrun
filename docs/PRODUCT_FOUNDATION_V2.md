This file preserves the existence and outcome of the TED-only product pivot. It must not be read as a current build instruction.

## Historical mechanism

The v2 pivot attempted to make already-published TED notices the primary commercial object:

`TED notice -> purchasable requirements -> supplier relevance -> evidence -> customer feed`

It was deliberately different from the original funded-project-first runway mechanism.

## Validation outcome

The broad v2 supplier-demand hypothesis did not pass its preregistered tests.

Phase 0B, 300 notices:

- any requirement: 26.0%;
- description-only value: 2.7% — FAIL against the frozen 10.0% gate;
- CPV-blind value: 20.7%;
- distinct categories: 20;
- domains represented: 5;
- overall result: FAIL.

Phase 0C, disjoint 300-notice period:

- any normalized requirement: 20.3%;
- CPV-blind value: 18.7%;
- distinct categories: 13 — FAIL against the frozen 15-category gate;
- domains represented: 5;
- overall result: FAIL.

No threshold is lowered and neither test is relabelled as PASS.

## What remains valid

TED Search API source qualification remains valid for the capabilities it actually proved:

- production-safe field-bounded procurement retrieval;
- active Portugal infrastructure procurement feed;
- historical procurement market intelligence;
- reproducible bounded CPV-blind text enrichment.

TED alone did not prove reliable early procurement runway or comprehensive EU-funding linkage.

## Current disposition

TED remains an approved procurement-evidence and market-context source inside the restored funded-project-first product. The CPV-blind signal may be used as bounded enrichment where exact evidence exists.

The following historical v2 claims are retired as the core product promise:

- that ProcRun's main commercial object is a TED opportunity;
- that the product differentiates primarily by extracting supplier demand from already-published notices;
- that TED-only country expansion is the central moat;
- that a notice feed should replace funded-project runway.

Current product semantics, routes, differentiation and readiness are defined only by `PRODUCT_FOUNDATION_FINAL.md` and `BUILD_GATES.md` A20.
